Principles for a Happy Life.

October 3, 2023

I spent one of my last days in Massachusetts swimming in a wooded lake with a close friend of mine. For hours we floated and talked. Not unlike the clouds and songbirds overhead.

Eventually, silence came upon us. I became pensive and asked, “What’s the secret to a happy life?”

My friend responded:

  1. Sleep as much as you want.
  2. Don’t hurt people.
  3. That’s it.
