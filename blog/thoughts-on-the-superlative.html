On the Superlative.

September 28, 2023

Lately I’ve grown skeptical of superlatives.

Is there such a thing as the best or greatest? We seem to have convinced ourselves that the superlative is real. That it’s a destination we can reach.

There is no superlative.

Only continuous improvement.
